Tracking and reducing WhatsApp service message costs

From 1 October 2026, Meta charges for service messages on WhatsApp. This SOP explains what changed, how we cut the spend, and how to check our costs every week.

1. What changed

Service messages were free until 30 Sep 2026. From 1 Oct 2026 they are billed at the Utility rate for the country.
  • A service message is any free-form reply sent inside the 24-hour customer service window. This includes replies from agents and replies from automation or AI bot journeys.
  • Messages the customer sends to us are not charged.
  • Utility template messages sent inside the 24-hour window were free before. They are now billed at Utility rates too.
  • Meta gives a free tier of 1,000 service messages per business phone number, per month.
  • Applies to WhatsApp only.

Why this matters for us

  • We sent 6,916 service messages in September. All of them would have been billable under the new rule.
  • The rate is similar to a Utility message, roughly ₹0.12 per message (confirm the exact rate in the BIK pricing article linked in section 6).
  • Website WhatsApp chat journeys (product enquiries, form prompts) trigger automated replies. Each one counts as a service message.

3. How we reduce the spend

  1. Use BIK Webforms for structured data Collect order details, preferences and addresses in one form instead of a back-and-forth chat. Candidates: return gift enquiries and bulk order forms. Current forms may not be linked to the WhatsApp journey yet, so this is an integration to explore.
  2. Consolidate replies Send one complete answer instead of splitting it across several messages. A service message can be up to 1,024 characters, so agents should fit the answer inside that limit.
  3. Route through the 72-hour free entry points Where the funnel allows it, start conversations from CTWA ads or Facebook CTAs.
  4. Move website support to Manifest AI Manifest AI can handle website product discovery and support without adding to WhatsApp costs.
Team awareness: Jai is responsible for making agents aware of message volume and for finding journeys that trigger multiple message queries.

4. How to check spend (daily or weekly)

Use this to see how much WhatsApp is costing us. You can run it for any date range, not only the current month.

  1. Click the profile button Top right corner, the round icon with your initials. The menu shows the usage wallet balance.
  2. Click View history It sits on the right of the Usage Wallet balance.
  3. Go to Billing & usage In Settings, open Billing & usage from the left menu, then stay on the Overview tab. The WhatsApp Messages card shows total messages delivered and total cost for the billing cycle.
  4. Click View breakdown Next to Total messages delivered. Set the date range you want (for example the last 7 days) and read the message count and cost for that period.

5. Weekly routine

  • Open View breakdown for the last 7 days and note message count and cost.
  • Compare with the previous week. Flag any rise.
  • Check the wallet balance and the days of usage left. Recharge before it runs low.
  • If service messages spike, find the journey or agent habit behind it and fix it with a form or consolidated reply.
  • Share the numbers with Jai so agent communication can be adjusted.
Meta fee waiver: the deadline to apply was 30 Sep 2026. Email help@bik.ai with the WhatsApp phone number, Facebook BM ID and BM name to ask whether a late request is possible.
